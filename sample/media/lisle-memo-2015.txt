To: Desmond Harlowe, Chief Executive, Vale Water Authority. From: Gareth Lisle, Inspector. Date: 2 June 2015.

On inspection of the Harrowmere spillway on 28 May 2015 I found cracking along the left wall, running from the second construction joint toward the top of the wall.

The cracking is wider than at the 2013 inspection. I recommend that the design certificate DC-0917 be reviewed and that the left wall be surveyed before the winter storms.

I ask that this memorandum be placed on the Authority's record.
