The story people want is concealment. The story the numbers tell is a design margin that was too thin for the storm of 14 March 2017.

The cracking Gareth Lisle reported in 2015 is the kind every spillway of that age shows. It does not prove anyone knew the wall would fail.

The loss of core sample C-14 is careless, not sinister: laboratories lose cores. The Long Table makes too much of it.

None of this excuses the Vale Water Authority. But the inquiry should judge the engineering first and the motives second.
