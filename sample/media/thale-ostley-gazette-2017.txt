Two years before the night of 14 March 2017, when the Harrowmere spillway failed and Ostley Vale flooded, an inspector of the Vale Water Authority warned his chief executive in writing that the left wall was cracking.

The memorandum, dated 2 June 2015 and seen by the Ostley Gazette, was written by Gareth Lisle. A source inside the Authority, who asked to be called Heron, said the memo was never placed on the record.

In March Desmond Harlowe, chief executive of the Vale Water Authority, said no warning had ever been put in writing. The Authority did not answer questions about the memo by the time of publication.

Calloway Ashdown Civil, which built the spillway in 2009, said it had not seen the memo.
