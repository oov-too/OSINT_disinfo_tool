Ed1888 Charter 1:1	The Commissioners shall hold the reservoir at Harrowmere and its works in trust for the inhabitants of Ostley Vale.
Ed1888 Charter 1:2	The Commissioners shall cause the dam, the spillway and every channel to be inspected once in each year.
Ed1888 Charter 1:3	Every inspection shall be reported in writing and entered in the book of the works.
Ed1888 Charter 2:1	No report of a defect shall be withdrawn from the book; a later report may answer it but shall not replace it.
Ed1888 Charter 2:2	Any inhabitant may read the book of the works at the office of the Commissioners.
