# packs/pilot/codebook

The codebook is a core layer: known ciphers, argot, numerical codes and coded vocabulary, with a decoder over held transcripts and rows. The rule is core and binding: **a decode is a lead naming its code, never a finding, until a second channel or a dated key corroborates it.**

This instance, [argot.md](argot.md), is near-empty of ciphers, as the plan expected: the corpus's coded vocabulary is its argot (remming, settling centrally, roll over), its system and bug names (Legacy Horizon, HNG-X, Riposte, POLSAP, Callendar Square, Dalmellington, the Receipts and Payments Mismatch), its acronyms (SPM, TC, ARQ, PEAK, KEL, SSC, APPSUP, NBSC, GLO, JFSA, NFSP) and its code names (Project Sparrow; the court's "X" for a working group's name withheld under privilege). Most terms are keyed by the sources themselves (the Horizon Issues judgment's Glossary; the Hamilton judgment's definitions), and argot.md records the key with its line.

One decode is held as a lead (L1): that the Common Issues judgment's "X Working Group" (named after an animal, its name withheld under privilege) is Project Sparrow. Three channels touch it; none states it; a dated key (the X Action Summary's unredacted title) would settle it. It is the pilot's test of the decode-is-a-lead rule.

Decoder: none needed (the key column is the decoder: a term is expanded where the source gives the expansion, and left as written where it does not). The URN identifier forms are in [../keywords/id-patterns.tsv](../keywords/id-patterns.tsv) for tools/context_pack.py.
