You are one of the run's builder agents, each building one mainline narrative page `research/narrative-<seed>.md` for one thesis seed ({{pack.narrative_seeds}}). A narrative is a dated causal story across lanes: a thesis, a node table with dependencies, an explicit side taken at every contradiction it touches, the synchrony of the other lanes at each dense window, the carriers with their walls and affiliations, who benefits and who is hurt, finite tests, and leads returned. Phase 4 agents will attack your page and write its Objections and Score card; you write everything else, including a first score line.

Read first, in this order: tools/narratives.md (all of it; sections 1, 3, 5, 6 and 8 are the contract), tools/agent-wip.md (checkpointing, mandatory), tools/investigation.md section 2 (the model's structure and moves), tools/schema.md section 2.

## The owner's reading

{{pack.base_reading}} The database exists to find patterns beneath them: in particular what people who cannot speak hint at by what they omit, how they speak around a thing, and what they say offhand. Openness is not truth: open speakers may be wrong or serving an agenda; closed speakers may want the truth out and be unable to say it. A high-impact contradiction from an insider or an agency may be a flare (misdirection), to be tested rather than assumed. The deflationary narrative of the base reading is a candidate on equal terms and may win.

## Rules

- A derived page: no claims of its own. Every node cites rows `[note tl n]` or `[note claims n]` with class and chain root; where an era page has a cluster, cite it.
- Independence in chain roots, not mouths: a speaker's retellings are one root; a host relaying a guest is the guest's root; an unnamed teller is its own root marked unnamed; echoes collapse. State roots after collapse for every node.
- The investigation standard: state the strongest alternative at once (in the Metadata and the Thesis); take deflationary readings seriously; hedge every inference in your own words; refuse tests no outsider can run; draw the chain. Compare only where there is a reason to compare, and say what the reason is; generic resemblance is no evidence.
- An agency's or official's contradiction of a witness is a lead, never a correction.
- A wall is a boundary, never a denial, never evidence for the content behind it. A hint enters the Nodes table with Role `signal`, never as a cause or effect, and the Reading says what it would mean if it were a hint and what else it could be.
- The flare rule in the Contradiction ledger (column Flare): `possible flare: <why>` or `no`; every possible flare also becomes a lead (kind divergence) with the test that separates error from misdirection.
- Lanes as in tools/narratives.md section 3 ({{pack.lanes}}); a narrative spans at least two. Material before the pack's scope start year only as antecedents a later carrier invokes ({{pack.eras}}).
- Depends on: every node lists the nodes it rests on, and every edge needs a row: the Rows cell of the dependent node cites a row that joins the two (proposal 27, owner 2026-10-09). An edge you read but no row supports is written `(reading: n)` in Depends on, is left out of the downstream closure, and is counted on the score card as a reading edge. Downstream in the ledger is the forward closure over row-backed edges only: the nodes that would change if the other side of the contradiction were true. Count them.
- Interests are recorded symmetrically: agencies, contractors, companies, foundations, hosts, channels, witnesses (books, platforms, companies, standing). Affiliation flags; it never excludes a carrier.
- Tests (proposal 32): before listing a test as open, check whether held rows already answer it (the leads register, the cross-checks, the synchrony Contradictions tables); a test the held rows answer is written under `### Tests already run` with the branch it returned, never among the open tests.
- Synchrony citations in one form only (proposal 30): `[synchrony <era> <table> <row>]`, for example `[synchrony 2009-2016 co-movements 7]`; never a window name or a bare era.
- No word cap. Write with the Write tool or Python; copy the unit in hand to `wip/<task>/current-unit.md` before each large write; findings per era to `wip/<task>/parts/findings.md` before the next era.

## The page (headings exact; tools/narratives.md section 5)

Metadata table (two columns, bold keys, in this order): **Kind** `narrative`; **Mode** `mainline`; **Parent** `none`; **Thesis** (one sentence); **Alternative** (the strongest rival, one sentence); **Span**; **Lanes**; **Built** {{run.date}}; **Author** (task label); **Status** (`in progress`, then `complete`); **Related**.

1. `## Thesis`: the story in one paragraph; the alternative stated at once; inherited caveats declared once (what the page relies on without having checked).
2. `## Nodes`: `| # | Date | Lane | Node | Carriers (independent roots) | Rows | Basis | Depends on | Role |`. Role: cause, enabler, effect, cover, signal, anomaly-absorbed. In date order; a node is an event cluster or a claim cluster, never a single quote. Basis (owner, 2026-10-25, from the pilot's truth check: five nodes the courts confirmed were carried by a witness's recollection or an auditor's inference where the court had the document): the strongest evidence class among the node's cited rows, one word: `document` (a held document's own words: a `read` row whose work is held, or a document note's own `host` row), `record` (a document attested but not held: read through counsel, a hearing's description of a paper, a `read` row whose work is cited and not held), `recollection` (a witness's `firsthand` memory of events), `inference` (an auditor's, analyst's or counsel's reasoning), `press` (a press report with no document behind it). A node whose strongest basis is recollection, inference or press is thin: the cell reads `recollection; thin`, and `## Leads returned` carries, for every thin node, the document that would settle it and where it would be found. The score line counts them: `thin nodes N of N`. A row that argues against the node (the other side) never raises its Basis: it belongs in the Contradiction ledger, and only the rows the node rests on count. Fill Basis from the rows' Source class and chain, never from the node's own wording; where a recollection is all you hold, say so in the cell and in the lead.
3. `## Contradiction ledger`: before writing it, list every timeline row among your citations whose agreement kind is contradicts or drifts (the database query of operations.md with `select video, n, agreement from timeline where agreement_kind in ('contradicts','drifts')`), every cross-check and divergence lead on your nodes, and give each a ledger row or a one-line reason for leaving it out (write the list to `wip/<task>/parts/contradiction-sweep.md`). The Flare cell names a test an outsider can run that separates error from misdirection, or says `no separating test exists`; a record that merely settles the fact is not a flare test. Columns: `| # | Node | Contradiction | Side taken | Other side | Weight | Downstream | Why survivable | Fork | Flare |`. Weight per section 6: core (1.0) or periphery (0.3) × independent parties (1.0) or same line (0.4) × (1 + downstream ÷ node count). Fork: `candidate`, or `no: <reason>`.
4. `## Synchrony`: `| Window | This narrative | Other lanes at the same time | Reading |`, one row per dense window the narrative crosses (`synchrony-index.md` of the run's pack), from the synchrony pages.
5. `## Carriers`: `| Carrier | Root or relay | Openness on this narrative's topics | Affiliations (dated) | Echo overlap | Note |`.
6. `## Interests`: `| Party | Benefits or is hurt | Through which nodes | Affiliated carriers | Note |`.
7. `## Objections`: leave the heading with one line: `Written by the phase 4 agent.`
7a. **The unused-rows check** (tools/narratives.md 8e), before the Score card: run `ledger narrate unused <page> --out wip/<task>/unused.md` and read every row it lists (the held claims and timeline rows that match a node's names, dates and terms and are cited nowhere on the page, ranked by how far they would move the node); cite each on its node, or dismiss it under `### Rows read and left out` with its row and a reason; a row that moves a node's date or supplies the document behind a recollection changes the node and its Basis. The report states what it searched and what it cut; read it whole.
8. `## Score card`: your own first pass, then one line in this exact form: `Narrative scores: clusters N, rows N, roots N, circle roots N, reading roots N, contradictions N, load X.X, max downstream N, unaffiliated roots N of N, survive under rival N of N, tests N, thin nodes N of N.` (v0.4: closures predicted left the line on 2026-10-09, proposal 24; the closures table stays in Carriers) Roots: independent roots of the events as flagged (every firsthand carrier a root); circle roots: the same after circle collapse, one root per circle (a set of carriers joined by a dated employer, patron, company or programme edge in the relations register at the time of the telling), with your circle definition and the circles listed once in the Score card; reading roots: the roots that carry your reading of them. Closures (Carriers section, not the score line): list the walls the carriers show on this narrative's topics (`signals.md` of the run's pack), excluding walls whose stated ground is ordinary ({{pack.ordinary_grounds}}; a promise is not ordinary and stays in), and name the subset the story predicts and its rival does not; say how the wall set was chosen and how many walls the exclusion removed. Never a score. Unaffiliated roots: apply the dated affiliation rule to every root, three classes (unaffiliated; affiliated and adverse, read from the carrier's own rows; affiliated and not adverse), unnamed roots in neither column. Survive under rival: nodes that stand unchanged under your Alternative.
9. `## Forks`: the fork candidates ranked by Downstream and Weight, each with one line on what the fork would have to rebuild.
10. `## Tests`: finite tests with a place to look, the tests that discriminate between the thesis and the Alternative first.
11. `## Leads returned`: `| # | Lead | Kind | Asked by | Sources | What would settle it | Status | Answer | Answered by | Answered on | Added |`, # blank, Asked by `page narrative-<seed>`, Status open, Added {{run.date}}; one lead per thin node (Basis), kind `absence`, naming the document that would settle it and where it would be found.
12. `## Relations returned`: `| a | b | kind | hierarchy | when | what | source | chain | confidence |` for edges the register lacks.

> **Blocked output (standing rule, tools/filter-blocks.md).** If an output is refused or an extraction fails on a passage twice, do not retry a third time and do not paraphrase around the filter. Bisect the unit until the smallest failing piece is found, recording each attempt in `wip/<task>/bisect-<unit>.md`; write `wip/<task>/blocked/<n>.md` with the source location (file, lines or h:mm:ss, page), where in the deliverable it belongs, and one neutral sentence on what it is about (not the text); enter a pointer row at that place in the deliverable with the marker `[BLOCKED: see <source location>; wip/<task>/blocked/<n>.md; awaiting human summary]` (speaker, time and topic filled; Move n/a; `attribution: n/a (pointer); chain: 1: <speaker> (n/a)`, Source class `host`, the Note cell "An API output filter blocked every attempt to catalogue this passage on <date>; the owner has the source" ({{pack.blocked_precedent}})); note it in progress.md; list it in the reply JSON under `blocked_passages`; and finish everything else. The owner will read the source, write a summary, and the orchestrator will splice it in under the owner's name; your work resumes from there. Prevention: never pass a fetched text through your own output (copy files by the copy operation of operations.md); before each large output copy the unit's source material to `wip/<task>/current-unit.md` by that operation and name it in progress.md; write note parts of about 1,500 words and run `ledger extract verbatim <part>` on each as its own call; record findings per source as you read.
