The judgement tests concentrate on what the diff shows changed or added; the audit covers the whole page mechanically, and any audit finding (missing citation, downstream mismatch, circle collapse) is a finding whether or not it is in the diff.

Agent A runs tests 1 (citations: every row the diff adds or changes, at least 40, read in one `--rows` batch: does the row say what the node or ledger row says?), 2 (independence on the ten nodes with the most carriers), 3 (the ledger: the sweep's missing rows; the other side's best evidence at the changed rows; weight and downstream recomputed for the five heaviest), 4 (flares), 5a (edges: the ten heaviest rows' edges read against their joining rows) and, for a fork, 5b (anomalies by root cluster, discriminating or not) and the deltas against the parent's newest attacker line.
Agent B runs tests 5 (the closures subset in Carriers), 6 (interests, `ledger analyse relations "<name>"`), 7 (the deflationary reading from the rival sheet `research/narrative-rival-<slug>.md`, listing rows for its `## Additions` in your findings file, not on the sheet), 8 (tests runnable, and tests the held rows already answer), and test 11 (the adoption check) where a Revision table exists: read `wip/<task>/adoptions.md`, the adoption check made by the tool before you started (tools/adoption_check.md: one block per adopted or partly adopted objection, severity first, with the objection's text, what the reviser changed, every Rows read row resolved and printed in full, the leads with the register's status, and the nodes and ledger rows the adoption changed with their citations set against the Rows read set). For each block rule whether the rows say what the objection says and whether the changed page text rests on the rows read or on rows the reviser did not name; report every FAIL line in the summary as a finding on its own (the revision rule was broken), whether or not the adoption is right. Do not re-derive the list from `ledger narrate narratives --section <page> "latest:Revision"`; use it only if `adoptions.md` is missing, and say so in your findings. Agent B also runs test 10 (the unused rows: `ledger narrate unused <page> --out wip/<task>/unused-attack.md`, every listed row read). Agent A also runs test 9 (the basis audit: every node's Basis recomputed from its rows; thin nodes counted).
