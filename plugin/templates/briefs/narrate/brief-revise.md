You revise one narrative page written in an earlier step (a mainline or a fork) so that it meets tools/narratives.md v0.3 and answers its attacker. You are the page's new author: you may change every section except `## Objections` and the attacker's score line, which stay exactly as written so the re-attacker can check your work against them.

Read first, in this order: tools/narratives.md (all; sections 5, 5a, 6, 8 and 10 are the contract, including the twelve changes adopted on 2026-10-08 and the circle rule), tools/agent-wip.md, tools/investigation.md section 2.2, `brief-builder.md` of this folder (the builders' rules, now v0.3) and, for a fork, `brief-fork.md` of this folder; then the page in full, its `## Objections` in full, and its attacker's score line.

## Tools first (token rule, 2026-10-09)

## What to do, in order
1. **Answer every objection, after checking it.** Before adopting an objection, verify the objection's own rows (the attacker can misread too: on 2026-10-09 a revision adopted an objection that made a host's reading of a document into a second insider, and re-broke a row and two nodes); an objection whose rows do not say what it says is answered `declined: <rows>` in the Revision table. For each numbered objection, do what it asks or say why not: repair or drop broken nodes; remove dependency edges with no row support; correct miscredited rows; restate the Alternative with its best rows where the attacker showed a strawman. Record each in `## Revision (<date>)` (new section, placed after `## Objections`): `| Objection | What changed | Rows read | Leads checked |` (proposal 25, owner 2026-10-09): Rows read lists every row you read to check the objection, Leads checked every lead on those rows and its status; an adopted objection with an empty Rows read cell fails the check. Where the attacker struck a rowless edge, keep it struck or supply the row (proposal 27). Where a test is already answered by held rows, move it under `### Tests already run` with its branch (proposal 32). For a fork, re-measure the Deltas against the parent's current attacker line before anything else (proposal 31).
2. **The contradiction sweep (change 30).** List every timeline row among your citations with agreement kind contradicts or drifts, every cross-check and divergence lead on your nodes, and the synchrony pages' Contradictions tables for your windows; give each a ledger row or a one-line reason for leaving it out (write the sweep to `wip/<task>/parts/contradiction-sweep.md`). New ledger rows take new numbers after the last; never renumber.
3. **Flares (change 31).** Every Flare cell names a test an outsider can run that separates error from misdirection, or says `no separating test exists`. Where a possible flare now has a document behind it ({{run.flare_documents}}), say what the document did to the flare.
4. **Fold in the new notes.** For every ledger row a new note addresses, update Side taken and Why survivable where the document or telling settles or shifts it, citing the note's rows; add nodes where the notes supply events the page lacked ({{run.new_events}}). Mark these rows `(revised {{run.date}})` in Why survivable.
5. **Carriers and interests (changes 27 and 29).** The wall set excludes ordinary grounds ({{pack.ordinary_grounds}}) and keeps promises; state how it was chosen and how many walls it removed. Affiliation in three classes, adversity read from the carrier's own rows; unnamed roots counted apart.
6. **Roots (changes 26 and 28).** Roots as flagged; circle roots after collapse with the circle definition and the circles listed once in the Score card; reading roots beside event roots.
7. **Survive under rival and tests (change 35).** State how many nodes survive unchanged under the Alternative; reorder Tests with the discriminating ones first; strike tests no outsider can run.
8. **Score card.** Keep the attacker's line as written and the original author line marked `author (v1):`; add `author (revised):` with the v0.3 line: `Narrative scores: clusters N, rows N, roots N, circle roots N, reading roots N, contradictions N, load X.X, max downstream N, closures predicted N of N, unaffiliated roots N of N, survive under rival N of N, tests N.` Run `ledger narrate narratives <page>` and reconcile every downstream count.
9. **Forks only (changes 32 to 34).** Carry the parent's attacker corrections first, listed under `### Carried from the parent's attacker` in `## Deltas against the parent`; measure deltas against the parent's attacker score line (the parent's own revision runs in parallel: cite the attacker line, and say so); keep one flare reading at the fork node through every node.
10. Metadata: **Status** `revised {{run.date}}`; add **Revised** with the task label.

Rules: derived page, every statement row-cited; independence in chain roots; an agency's contradiction is a lead not a correction; openness is not truth; hints stay signals; no word cap; write with the Edit tool or Python (exact string replacement), checkpoint from the first minute (tools/agent-wip.md), the blocked-output rule (tools/filter-blocks.md section 5). Return new leads and relations in `## Leads returned` and `## Relations returned` (append; `#` blank for new rows).

Reply JSON, also at `wip/<task>/report.json`: `{"page": "...", "objections_answered": N, "objections_declined": N, "nodes_repaired": N, "nodes_dropped": N, "edges_removed": N, "ledger_rows_added": N, "flares_rewritten": N, "rows_revised_from_new_notes": N, "score_line_revised": "...", "leads": N, "relations": N, "notes": "..."}`.

## WIP, resume and output errors (standing)
Follow tools/agent-wip.md: `progress.md` from the first minute with the step reached and the next step; the deliverable saved after every step; `report.json` built incrementally; `current-unit.md` before every large write (by the copy operation of operations.md); on any resume read your wip folder first and continue from the last completed step, never from scratch. Tool outputs you are given (digest, sweep, interests seed, audit) are files: read them with the Read tool; never pass their text, or any source text, through your own reply. Before replying run `ledger extract report-check <your report.json> --form <form>` and fix every problem it lists; a failing report is never sent. {{pack.model_policy}}

> **Blocked output (standing rule, tools/filter-blocks.md).** If an output is refused or an extraction fails on a passage twice, do not retry a third time and do not paraphrase around the filter. Bisect the unit until the smallest failing piece is found, recording each attempt in `wip/<task>/bisect-<unit>.md`; write `wip/<task>/blocked/<n>.md` with the source location (file, lines or h:mm:ss, page), where in the deliverable it belongs, and one neutral sentence on what it is about (not the text); enter a pointer row at that place in the deliverable with the marker `[BLOCKED: see <source location>; wip/<task>/blocked/<n>.md; awaiting human summary]` (speaker, time and topic filled; Move n/a; `attribution: n/a (pointer); chain: 1: <speaker> (n/a)`, Source class `host`, the Note cell "An API output filter blocked every attempt to catalogue this passage on <date>; the owner has the source" ({{pack.blocked_precedent}})); note it in progress.md; list it in the reply JSON under `blocked_passages`; and finish everything else. The owner will read the source, write a summary, and the orchestrator will splice it in under the owner's name; your work resumes from there. Prevention: never pass a fetched text through your own output (copy files by the copy operation of operations.md); before each large output copy the unit's source material to `wip/<task>/current-unit.md` by that operation and name it in progress.md; write note parts of about 1,500 words and run `ledger extract verbatim <part>` on each as its own call; record findings per source as you read.
